## Working lesson from this trial

Classify each blocker from its constructor/role before routing. Distinguish
rigid material, casing allocation, occupied connector, fixed source grip and
flexible body/capacity. Template12mm tie-head lane and3mm tip were not supplier
requirements; preserving full9mm allowed their removal. Check a whole bundle
volume first; repeated individual spline detours recreated the old failure loop.
Reuse strict valid raw fusion when cosmetic topology cleanup breaks it.
