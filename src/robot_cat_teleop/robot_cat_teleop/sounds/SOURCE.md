# meow.wav

Trimmed from **"Meow of a pleading cat"** by *Heismark*, via Wikimedia Commons:

<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Meow_of_a_pleading_cat.oga>

**Licence: public domain.** No attribution is required; this note records the
provenance so nobody has to re-derive it.

The source is an 11.9 s Ogg Vorbis recording of six meows at 8 kHz mono. What
ships here is the third of them (3.66 s - 4.82 s), decoded to 16-bit PCM WAV,
faded 12 ms at each end to kill the cut click, and peak-normalised to 90%.
`afplay` cannot play Ogg, hence the conversion.
